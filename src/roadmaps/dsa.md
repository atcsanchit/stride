# DSA interview path

Follow this in order. Topic sequence is Striver's A2Z sheet. Problems are the ones that keep showing up across the lists people actually finish: Striver SDE Sheet (191), NeetCode 150, Blind 75, and LeetCode Top Interview 150. A2Z's 474 includes beginner C++/STL drills — skipped. Love Babbar 450 is the same core with more GFG volume; do not grind both.

**How to learn anything (Erin Meryl):** watching a solution is familiarity, not learning. Each session is production: blurt the pattern, code from a blank editor, then write one sentence of what you learned. Revisit within 24 hours on Revise. A day only counts after you Complete.

Finish a section before hopping. Log a problem when you can explain the approach without the editor.

## How to learn anything (session)
- [ ] Blurt the pattern for 10 minutes before opening a solution or the class notes
- [ ] Code one problem from a blank Lab — no solution tab until you are stuck
- [ ] Write one sentence of what you actually learned (Complete notes). That is how you follow the interest
- [ ] Revisit within 24 hours on Revise: retrieve first, then check holes
- [ ] Lower activation energy: open Lab and type a function signature in two minutes when you do not want to start
- [ ] Weekly contest or timed set: apply, do not grind a new playlist
